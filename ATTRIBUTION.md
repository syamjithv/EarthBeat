# Attribution

EarthBeat uses NASA resources:

- NASA GISS Surface Temperature Analysis (GISTEMP v4), global monthly
  Land-Ocean Temperature Index anomalies.
- NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, “NASA Climate Spiral 1880–2022,”
  SVS 5057.
- NASA Earth Information Center, referenced through the NASA Space Apps
  Challenge brief: <https://www.nasa.gov/earth-information-center/>.

NASA data and visualization remain NASA resources. EarthBeat supplies an
independent interface and a generated sonification layer. The sonification is
an interpretive translation of measurements, not a NASA-authored audio product.
